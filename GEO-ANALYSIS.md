# TennisFriends GEO/AEO Analysis

Analyzed: 2026-08-27
Scope: repository source and generated search artifacts; no external brand-mention API was used.

## Readiness score

- GEO readiness: 80/100 after the changes in this workstream.
- Google AI Overviews: 82/100. Static HTML, sitemap coverage, canonical metadata, structured data, and answer-first homepage copy are present; author/reviewer depth and source coverage still vary by article.
- ChatGPT search: 80/100. GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, and ChatGPT-User are explicitly allowed and first-party AI discovery files are published; third-party entity mentions were not verified.
- Perplexity: 78/100. PerplexityBot is allowed and the site exposes `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, RSS, sitemaps, and a structured AI index; off-site community validation was not checked.

## Technical accessibility

- Key routes are generated by Next.js and remain readable without requiring client-side JavaScript for primary content.
- `/robots.txt` explicitly allows GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, CCBot, and cohere-ai while keeping private and admin paths out of the general crawl surface.
- `/.well-known/ai.txt`, `/.well-known/robots.txt`, `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`, and `/ai-index.json` provide redundant discovery paths.
- The 2026-08-27 sitemap audit found 1,465 entries with no coverage findings. The curated AI index now matches the same 1,133 indexable blog articles and contains 1,467 entries including its document mirrors.

## Entity and structured-data status

- The site-wide `Organization` and `WebSite` entities now use stable `#organization` and `#website` identifiers.
- Blog authorship is represented as the TennisFriends editorial organization instead of a fictional `Person` entity.
- Dynamic blog pages retain one dedicated `BreadcrumbList`; the blog schema graph no longer emits a duplicate breadcrumb entity.
- Organization descriptions and publisher references are connected consistently across the root and article graphs.

## Passage-level citability and AEO

- The homepage now answers “TennisFriends에서는 무엇을 할 수 있나요?” immediately in a self-contained summary, links each claim to the relevant tool, and states the NTRP limitation plainly.
- Existing FAQ blocks provide question-and-answer phrasing. FAQPage markup is already present; it should be retained for machine understanding, not treated as a guaranteed Google rich-result feature.
- Article pages expose publication information, byline context, headings, related links, and BlogPosting data. Future editorial work should preserve direct answers near the beginning of each major section and cite primary sources for changing facts.

## Brand mention status

Repository evidence confirms the TennisFriends organization entity and first-party site references. Wikipedia, Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, and third-party citation presence were not checked in this local implementation pass, so no off-site visibility claim is made.
